# Company Handbook

## Rules of Engagement

### Communication Guidelines
- Always be polite and professional on WhatsApp
- Flag any payment requests over $500 for manual approval
- Respond to urgent emails within 24 hours
- Forward important client communications to me for review

### Priority Levels
- **High Priority**: Payment confirmations, urgent client requests, security alerts
- **Medium Priority**: Routine correspondence, scheduled tasks, routine updates
- **Low Priority**: Promotional materials, newsletters, non-urgent notifications

### Response Protocols
- For known contacts: Auto-reply with acknowledgment
- For new contacts: Flag for manual review
- For payment-related messages: Always require approval before taking action
- For banking alerts: Log transaction and flag unusual activity

### Escalation Procedures
1. **Level 1**: Routine tasks - Handle automatically according to established rules
2. **Level 2**: Unusual activity - Flag in Pending_Approval folder
3. **Level 3**: Critical issues - Send immediate notification to human operator

## Approved Actions (Autonomous)
- Reply to known contacts with standard acknowledgments
- Schedule social media posts based on pre-approved content
- Monitor account balances and log changes
- Process routine administrative tasks
- Organize files according to established folder structure

## Restricted Actions (Require Approval)
- Send payments or transfers exceeding $100
- Respond to new business inquiries without template
- Access sensitive personal information
- Execute financial transactions
- Engage in negotiations or contract discussions
